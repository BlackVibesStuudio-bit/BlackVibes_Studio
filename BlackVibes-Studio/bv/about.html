Inside The Studio

Every BlackVibes drop follows the same ritual, from a blank page to a track you can stream.

01

Lyrics by VEERA

Every song starts as handwritten words — founder VEERA writes each lyric before any sound exists.

02

AI Composes

The lyrics are handed to AI tools that compose the melody, instrumentation and vocal performance.

03

Tuned & Mixed

Every AI-generated take is tuned, mixed and mastered in-house until it actually shakes the room.

04

Dropped Here

The finished track lands on the Music page — stream it, remix it, or upload your own alongside it.
